NAACP (National Association for the Advancement of Colored People) - Promotes civil rights for African Americans and advocates for social justice. Website: www.naacp.org

Black Lives Matter - A global organization that fights against systemic racism and violence towards Black people. Website: www.blacklivesmatter.com

Equal Justice Initiative - Provides legal representation to people who have been wrongly convicted or unfairly treated in the criminal justice system, with a focus on ending mass incarceration and excessive punishment. Website: www.eji.org

Color of Change - A racial justice organization that works to hold corporations and government accountable for their actions and policies that disproportionately impact Black communities. Website: www.colorofchange.org

The Southern Poverty Law Center - Monitors hate groups and extremist organizations and works to combat bigotry and discrimination. Website: www.splcenter.org

The National Council of La Raza - The largest national Hispanic civil rights and advocacy organization in the United States, working to improve opportunities for Latinos. Website: www.nclr.org

The Asian Pacific American Legal Center - A civil rights organization that promotes justice for Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders. Website: www.apalc.org

The National Congress of American Indians - The oldest and largest national organization representing the interests of Native American and Alaska Native tribes. Website: www.ncai.org
The Trevor Project - A crisis intervention and suicide prevention organization for LGBTQ+ young people. Website: www.thetrevorproject.org

The Anti-Defamation League - A civil rights organization that fights against anti-Semitism and all forms of hate. Website: www.adl.org
